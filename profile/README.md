## About

Slow Down 4 Sam is a non-profit organization based in Michigan. We are committed to promoting motorcycle safety education, particularly for young adults, in memory of Sam Gross.

Our mission is to help prevent motorcycle-related tragedies through education, awareness, and community outreach. We believe that teaching safe riding practices can save lives and honor the memory of those lost too soon.

## Mission

To educate young adults about motorcycle safety, encourage responsible riding habits, and foster a culture of awareness on Michigan roads — all in honor of Sam Gross.
